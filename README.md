## Favorite Series: Fight for My Way

> "Go ahead and make trouble. We're young enough to get away with it."

**Fight for My Way** is a South Korean drama about four longtime friends chasing their dreams despite realistic struggles in adulthood.

### Main Characters
* **Ko Dong-man** – A former *taekwondo* athlete making a comeback in MMA.
* **Choi Ae-ra** – An aspiring TV announcer working at a department store.
* **Kim Joo-man** & **Baek Seol-hee** – A couple navigating a 6-year relationship.

### Why It Stands Out
1. Relatable underdog storyline
2. Great comedic chemistry
3. Inspiring martial arts comeback arc
